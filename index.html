A Little About Me

Design is a way
of paying attention.

Hi, it's Joselyn! I'm currently a Senior at Parsons School of Design majoring in Communication Design. As a designer interested in the ways design can hold stories, preserve memories, and make people feel something.

My practice moves between editorial design, typography, printed matter, and visual experimentation. I'm drawn to thoughtful details, meaningful objects, and the relationship between what we see and what we remember.

I'm continuing to discover my voice as a designer, with a growing interest in elegant, poetic, and concept-driven visual work.
